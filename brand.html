Step 4 · Verbal Identity & Messaging Rules

Brand Voice & “Say This, Not That”

Full guidelines documented in 01-brand-identity/brand-guidelines.md.

3 Core Personality Pillars

  • 1. Warm & Neighbourly (Not Clinical): We sound like Andy & Geoff sitting at your kitchen table—never a hospital bureaucracy or a franchise call centre.
  • 2. Dignified & Adult-to-Adult (Not Patronizing): Older adults are autonomous people with rich lives and routines. We partner with them, never talk down to them.
  • 3. Dependable & Unhurried (Not Transactional): We emphasize relationship continuity—the same familiar face every week.
